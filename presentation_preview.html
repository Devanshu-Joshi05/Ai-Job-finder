"Good morning/afternoon, Respected Teacher!
Today I am presenting our project TalentAI – Smart AI Job Finder & Career Growth Platform.

The Problem: Freshers and college students face two huge hurdles when searching for jobs: (1) Fake job scams charging registration or laptop fees, and (2) 75%+ ATS resume rejections due to missing keywords.

Our Solution has 4 Core Innovations:
• 102+ Verified Corporate Jobs: Direct feeds from Google, Swiggy, Zomato, TCS, and Amazon with 1-click external apply links.
• AI Scam Detector: Automated heuristics that detect registration fees and Telegram redirects, warning students with a red risk badge.
• ATS Resume Optimizer: Compares student skills against job prerequisites, computes a real-time Match %, and generates a clean, downloadable ATS PDF.
• Kanban Tracker: Tracks stages (Applied, Review, Interview, Offer) with confetti celebrations and milestone alerts.

Technology & Deployment: Built using Tailwind CSS, vanilla modern JavaScript, Capacitor for Android APK packaging, and deployed live at talentai-rohan.surge.sh."

SLIDE 04 & 05 SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ALGORITHMS

Architecture & Technical Innovation

1. Scam Classifier Rule Engine:

Scans token vocabulary for phrases like "security deposit", "registration fee", "pay before test" and inspects contact URLs for unofficial domains.

2. Skill Match & Jaccard Scoring:

Calculates similarity index between applicant skills (React, Node, SQL) and job prerequisites, outputting an instant 0–100% score.

3. Multi-User Storage Isolation:

Employs partitioned local storage keys (talentai_saved_<email>) so different accounts on the same device never leak saved jobs or tracker stages.
